MINICRAFT
An accurate blocky sandbox — best on tablet • touch ready • 3D
Goal: explore, mine ⛏ and build 🧱. Crosshair block is targeted.
Desktop: WASD + mouse, Space jump, F toggle fly, LMB/RMB break/place.
Tablet: left joystick moves, drag right side to look around, use big ⛏ / 🧱 buttons. Push stick fully to sprint. Jump twice quickly (or 🕊) to fly; while flying hold JUMP to rise, joystick still moves.
Works offline after first load. Progress auto-saves locally.
Tip: rotate tablet to landscape for the best view.
